I'm building Realmglass with and for my son. It grew out of running Jaedenar, my Minecraft server. I wanted a public status page where players could check which worlds were online without having to open Minecraft.

The Jaedenar dashboard is the working example. Realmglass is the project to make that dashboard reusable for other self-hosted Minecraft Java and Bedrock servers.

[See the dashboard for Jaedenar](https://jaedenar.mcm.cx)
